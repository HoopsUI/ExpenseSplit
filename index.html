    💙 Why I Built ExpenseSplit

    Hi, and welcome to ExpenseSplit.

    ExpenseSplit started from a problem I've seen again and again in everyday life. Whether it was splitting restaurant bills with friends, dividing rent and utilities with roommates, or managing costs during group trips, the same question always seemed to come up: "Who owes whom?"

    What should have been a simple calculation often became confusing. Someone paid for dinner, someone else booked the accommodation, another person covered fuel costs, and before long people were scrolling through messages trying to figure everything out.

    I created ExpenseSplit because I wanted a simpler solution.

    Instead of forcing people to download another app, create accounts, or learn complicated software, I wanted to build practical tools that solve specific expense-sharing problems quickly and clearly.

    Today, ExpenseSplit includes calculators and guides for trips, roommates, restaurants, grocery sharing, subscriptions, weddings, and many other situations where people need a fair way to divide costs.

    My goal is simple: help people spend less time calculating expenses and more time enjoying the experiences they're sharing.

    Thank you for visiting ExpenseSplit and supporting this project.

    — Founder, ExpenseSplit

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    1. How do I split expenses between friends?
    Add everyone involved, enter each shared expense and select who paid. ExpenseSplit then calculates the group cost and each person's share. For more complicated payments, you can also use the Who Owes Whom Calculator.
    2. Can I split expenses online for free?
    Yes. ExpenseSplit provides free online expense split calculators that work directly in your browser without requiring an account or app download.
    3. How do I calculate who owes whom?
    Enter the people involved and record the expenses that each person paid. You can then use the Who Owes Whom Calculator to work out the payments needed to settle shared expenses.
    4. Can I split a bill when everyone paid different amounts?
    Yes. When people contribute different amounts, use an uneven or contribution-based split rather than assuming everyone should pay the same amount.
    5. Can I use the calculator for trips, restaurants and roommates?
    Yes. ExpenseSplit has specialized calculators for group trips, restaurant bills, roommates, Airbnb stays, gas costs, groceries, subscriptions and other shared expenses.
    6. Do I need an account to use the expense splitter?
    No. The calculator works directly in your browser and does not require an account or signup.
